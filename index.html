The problem we are solving

On a large campus, students often do not know who to tell about a problem, and once they report it, they cannot see what happened next.

❓

No clear channel

Complaints get lost between phone calls, notes and word of mouth.

🕑

No tracking

Students cannot see whether anyone has even looked at their issue.

📊

No overview

Staff cannot easily see which problems are most urgent or repeated.

About this project

CampusFix is a CA1 problem-statement project built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Data is stored in the browser using localStorage, so no server is needed. Future scope: a real backend with a database, email notifications and photo uploads.
